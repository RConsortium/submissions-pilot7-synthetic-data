PROTOCOL EXTRACT — ADVN CATH 03-01 v3.0 (July 7, 2009)
"Antimicrobial Response to Oral Vitamin D3 in Patients with Psoriasis"
Sponsor: NIAID. Single-center (UCSD). Phase 2.
Source: user-provided protocol PDF (in conversation). Key facts for the SCM/CRF build.

=== DESIGN (protocol §7.1.2, synopsis) ===
Single-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled.
Three diagnostic groups: psoriasis subjects, atopic dermatitis (AD) subjects, non-AD healthy controls.
Psoriatic subjects randomized 1:1 to oral Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol, 4000 IU/day) or matching
placebo for 21 days. AD and non-AD controls are enrolled from the main ADVN CATH 03 study and (in the
substudy) undergo tape stripping, microbial evaluation, and skin swabs; their Vitamin D3/placebo
administration, biopsies, saliva and blood are handled through the main study.
[NOTE: the posted ClinicalTrials.gov results report all three groups under both Vitamin D3 and
placebo arms — 6 cells, N=82 — which is what this build reproduces.]

=== SCHEDULE OF ACTIVITIES (protocol §10.6; Table 10.1 psoriatic, Table 10.2 AD/non-AD) ===
Study Visit 1 (Screening), Day -7 to -10:
  Informed consent, medical history, medication history, diagnosis of psoriasis, pregnancy test,
  blood draw [vitamin D 25-OH, serum total IgE, serum PTH, RAST, creatinine, calcium, serum storage].
Study Visit 2 (Baseline), Day 0 — RANDOMIZATION + start of study drug:
  Physical & oral exam, Fitzpatrick skin scale, PASI scoring, vital signs, pregnancy test,
  4 skin punch biopsies (2 lesional + 2 non-lesional; non-AD = non-lesional only),
  2 sets tape stripping (lesional + non-lesional), saliva collection, microbial/bacterial colony
  sampling, skin swab (subset, >=6/group). Medication history.
Study Visit 3, Day 21 (minimum Day 18, maximum Day 27) — end of dosing:
  Repeat physical & oral exam, PASI, vital signs, pregnancy test, repeat 4 skin punch biopsies,
  2 sets tape stripping, saliva, microbial sampling, skin swab (subset),
  blood draw [vitamin D 25-OH, serum total IgE, serum PTH, RAST, creatinine, calcium, serum storage].
Unscheduled Visit: repeat sample collection / AE monitoring / confirm results.

=== TREATMENT (protocol §9) ===
Vitamin D3 4000 IU/day PO x 21 days (24 capsules dispensed); placebo identical (microcrystalline
cellulose + fumed silica). Compliance by returned pill count at Visit 3. Re-dose window to Day 27.

=== ENDPOINTS (protocol §7.1.1 / CTGov outcomeMeasures) ===
PRIMARY: change baseline->Day21 in relative mRNA abundance of CAMP (cathelicidin/LL-37) and HBD-3
  (human beta-defensin-3) in lesional and non-lesional skin biopsies, Vitamin D3 vs placebo, within
  AD, non-AD and psoriasis groups. (qRT-PCR, normalized; CTGov unit "Cycle Number".)
SECONDARY: change baseline->Day21 in IL-13 mRNA (TH2 cytokine) in skin, same groups/arms.
  Additional protocol assessments: saliva cathelicidin, tape-stripping cathelicidin vs punch-biopsy,
  serum total IgE & RAST, PASI score, bacterial colony counts, skin-swab genomic flora (stored).

=== ELIGIBILITY (protocol §8) ===
Age 18-70; US residents; psoriasis = definitive plaque psoriasis >=6 months (ADVN criteria) OR
AD/non-AD control from main study. Exclusions incl. erythrodermic/guttate/pustular psoriasis,
pregnancy/lactation, diabetes, kidney disease/stones, hyperparathyroidism/sarcoid/TB/lymphoma,
vitamin D supplements >400 IU/day or other listed interacting meds, abnormal screening
calcium/PTH/creatinine, phototherapy or systemic immunomodulators within 30 days.

=== SAFETY (protocol §9.2.3, §16) ===
Oral Vitamin D3 4000 IU/day reported to raise serum 25-OH-D while maintaining normal calcium;
toxicity (hypercalcemia) only at far higher chronic doses (>=10,000-50,000 IU/day). Mild GI
complaints (constipation, gas, bloating, nausea) possible but no more frequent than non-users.
Procedure risks: skin biopsy (pain, rare infection/scar, rare lidocaine allergy), tape stripping
(transient mild erythema), blood draw, microbial evaluation (mild irritation). Stopping rule:
one Grade 4 or two Grade 3 AEs, or >=2 similar SAEs.
[CTGov posted result: 0 serious AEs, no AE at >=5% frequency, 0 deaths.]
