# Review guide

Review one AI-reported finding against the submitted Define-XML, the
reproduced yamaa specification and the ADaM data. No installation is needed;
one evidence-backed recommendation is a complete contribution. Results are
drafts pending human review.

## 1. Choose a finding

Open the [Pilot 5 consistency findings](../studies/submission-pilot5.md#submission-pilot5/overview/ADSL?findingSearch=ADSL)
and select **Start review** on a finding. Start with **Critical finding:
Yes** or **Needs review**. Use **By dataset** to choose another dataset.

## 2. Check the evidence

Read **The finding** and **Question for you**. Compare the cited Define-XML
item with the reproduced yamaa specification, then check **Subject examples**
for the reported difference. Use **View source** to inspect a citation.

Decide whether the evidence supports the finding and its criticality. A
100% reproduced ADaM dataset agreement does not resolve a disagreement
between the data and Define-XML.

## 3. Submit your assessment

Under **Human Review**, choose **Agree**, **Disagree** or **Need
clarification**, then **Critical**, **Not critical** or **Unsure**, and
explain your reasoning. Drafts save automatically in this browser.

Select **Submit review on GitHub** after both decisions. Sign in if asked,
check the prefilled assessment, then select **Start discussion** to publish
it. Opening the GitHub form does not submit your review.

[Open the pilot study report](../studies/index.md) to review another pilot study.
