# BNT162-01

**Pfizer/BioNTech**, BNT162b2 (Comirnaty). A Phase I/II, two-part dose-escalation trial in healthy adults of four RNA vaccine candidates (BNT162a1, BNT162b1, BNT162b2 and BNT162c2), in cohorts of 12. PHMPT released two interim cuts, each sequence S1 of a different application: EUA 27034 (cutoff 13 Aug 2020, 144 participants in cohorts 1 to 7) and BLA 125742 (cutoff 23 Oct 2020, 216 participants in cohorts 1 to 10, which add adults aged 56 to 85).

Specs: [`phmpt/bnt162-01`](https://github.com/RConsortium/submissions-pilot7-synthetic-data/tree/main/phmpt/bnt162-01) - yamaa `67b14f9` - results collected 2026-11-26 - terms: [glossary](../glossary.md)

Submitted data, programs and defines are not published here. Each result can be rerun from PHMPT with the published kit ([commands for this study](../reproduce.md#bnt162-01)).

**How datasets are compared.** yamaa's output is matched to the submitted dataset row by row on the dataset's keys and compared cell by cell. In this study numbers must be equal, strings must be equal, spaces included, and submitted cells FDA redacted to `(b)(6)` are not compared ([comparison rules](../glossary.md#comparison-rules)).

**Exact** counts datasets equal to the submitted one, strictly or under the comparison rules. **Open** counts datasets that still differ. Define-XML counts the received elements of datasets with a spec that are reproduced, exactly or apart from a named [engine gap](../glossary.md#engine-gap); *unexplained* would be any other difference.
