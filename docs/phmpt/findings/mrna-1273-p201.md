# mRNA-1273-P201

**Moderna**, mRNA-1273 (Spikevax). A Phase 2a dose-confirmation trial of mRNA-1273 in adults 18 years and older: 600 participants were randomized, observer-blind, to placebo or to mRNA-1273 at 50 or 100 micrograms, 200 per arm. PHMPT released two analysis cuts, both in BLA 125752 sequence S3: the Day 57 analysis and Addendum 1, the analysis at the end of Part A.

Specs: [`phmpt/mrna-1273-p201`](https://github.com/RConsortium/submissions-pilot7-synthetic-data/tree/main/phmpt/mrna-1273-p201) - yamaa `ac826f0` - results collected 2026-11-27 - terms: [glossary](../glossary.md)

Submitted data, programs and defines are not published here. To rerun this study from PHMPT, follow [`phmpt/reproduce.sh`](https://github.com/RConsortium/submissions-pilot7-synthetic-data/blob/main/phmpt/reproduce.sh): `sh phmpt/reproduce.sh setup`, then `sh phmpt/reproduce.sh mrna-1273-p201`.

**How datasets are compared.** yamaa's output is matched to the submitted dataset row by row on the dataset's keys and compared cell by cell. In this study numbers are equal within 1e-10, strings are compared with spaces removed, and submitted cells FDA redacted to `(b)(6)` are not compared ([comparison rules](../glossary.md#comparison-rules)).
