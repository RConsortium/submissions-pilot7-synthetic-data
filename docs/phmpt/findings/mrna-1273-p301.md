# mRNA-1273-P301

**Moderna**, mRNA-1273 (Spikevax). The Phase 3 efficacy trial of mRNA-1273 (COVE) in adults 18 years and older, placebo-controlled and observer-blind: 30,423 participants were randomized to mRNA-1273 100 micrograms or placebo, about 15,200 per arm. PHMPT released three separate submissions at different data cutoffs: the first interim analysis as IA1a (19745-83 S80, cutoff 11 Nov 2020) and IA1b (27073 S5, cutoff 25 Nov 2020), and the BLA interim analysis (BLA 125752 S3, cutoff 26 Mar 2021).

Specs: [`phmpt/mrna-1273-p301`](https://github.com/RConsortium/submissions-pilot7-synthetic-data/tree/main/phmpt/mrna-1273-p301) - yamaa `ac826f0` - results collected 2026-11-27 - terms: [glossary](../glossary.md)

Submitted data, programs and defines are not published here. To rerun this study from PHMPT, follow [`phmpt/reproduce.sh`](https://github.com/RConsortium/submissions-pilot7-synthetic-data/blob/main/phmpt/reproduce.sh): `sh phmpt/reproduce.sh setup`, then `sh phmpt/reproduce.sh mrna-1273-p301`.

**How datasets are compared.** yamaa's output is matched to the submitted dataset row by row on the dataset's keys and compared cell by cell. In this study numbers are equal within 1e-10, strings are compared with spaces removed, and submitted cells FDA redacted to `(b)(6)` are not compared ([comparison rules](../glossary.md#comparison-rules)).

### Differences and their reasons

**Missing input** ([glossary](../glossary.md#missing-input)). The release lacks an input these values are derived from. The spec derives them as the program does; the listed cells cannot be recovered from the released data.

- **ADCM**: 24 columns are not derived. *Missing:* CM variables the released S3 CM (a JMP conversion with 11 of its 24 variables) drops: CMSEQ, CMCAT, CMDECOD, CMINDC, CMCLAS, CMDOSE, CMDOSU, CMDOSFRQ, CMROUTE; the sponsor file import.pain_drugcode, not submitted; and the CMSTDTC values of 11 rows, redacted to (b)(6). Without CMSEQ no CM record can be joined to its SUPPCM records, and CMSEQ cannot be rebuilt: the released rows are sorted by start date and subject, and CMSEQ follows the sponsor's entry order, which no released variable carries. So the CM-coded columns, the SUPPCM columns (ATC levels, CMDECOD1-3, the dose, frequency and route specifications, CMSOLFL, CMPROTCD, CMTRTO1) and the flags built on them (CMMOI1FL-3FL) cannot be derived; CMMOI2FL also needs the sponsor's pain drug-code list. The rows are identified by a record number in their place. The start date imputation flag of 11 rows comes from a CMSTDTC redacted to (b)(6). The other columns were not compared cell by cell: the comparison stops when the column sets differ.
- **ADEX**: 2 cells differ (`NDOSRSP` 2). *Missing:* the unredacted SUPPEC reason text, and the record behind one '.' value. One reason text was redacted differently in the released SDTM (SUPPEC) and ADaM files, and one submitted value is "." with no SUPPEC record behind it (the only "." among 85,233 rows), so neither can be derived from the released SDTM. Every other cell is reproduced.
- **ADSL**: 8,528 cells differ (`RANDIGFL` 1,591, `PPSIFL` 1,457, `CCIASFL` 3,837, `DCSREASP` 4, `DCT01RSP` 5, `PPSIEREA` 1,634). *Missing:* the sponsor's files here.ccias_unblinded, here.random_list and here.additional_subcohort_unblinded, not submitted; the unredacted DS DSTERM of nine discontinuation reasons. RANDIGFL, CCIASFL, PPSIFL and PPSIEREA come from the immunogenicity subcohort lists the program reads from the sponsor's here library (RANDSFL and the subcohort membership), which are not in the release; the spec derives them as the program does with those files empty, so no subject is in a subcohort. In nine DCSREASP and DCT01RSP values the released DS ends a redacted DSTERM in "(b)(6)." where the submitted ADSL ends in "(b)(6)": FDA redacted the two files differently. Every other cell is reproduced.
- **ADVS**: 21 cells differ (`ATOXGRH` 7, `ATOXGRHN` 7, `SHIFT2` 7). *Missing:* the sponsor's file import.vsgrade4, not submitted. These rows are graded 4 in the submitted data only through the sponsor's external file import.vsgrade4, which is not in the release; from the released VS alone they grade 3. Every other cell is reproduced.

**Program and submitted file disagree** ([glossary](../glossary.md#program-note)). The released program's text and the submitted file disagree. These notes do not change the verdict.

- **ADSLSF**: a11adslsf.sas keeps one record per screen-failure date (by usubjid subjid dsstdtc; if last.dsstdtc), so the two subjects who failed screening on two dates get two records each, while the program's own note says the last reason is kept. The submitted ADSLSF has one record for each, the later one; the spec follows the note.
